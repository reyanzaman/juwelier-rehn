# Juwelier C. Rehn — production brief

## Approved direction

**Ewige Verbindung / Eternal Connection** — approved by the user.

The experience treats the two supplied rings as two individual signatures: distinct in appearance, deliberately connected, and finally held in balanced suspension. The film remains the emotional centerpiece and hands off into a complete German-first commercial website.

## Film law

- Exactly two rings throughout: one floral engraved ring and one wide polished ring.
- Preserve ring geometry, stones, engraving, champagne rose-gold color, proportions, light ivory environment, and controlled lighting.
- No extra jewelry, people, hands, boxes, flowers, fabric, particles, writing, hallmark, logo, watermark, or baked-in interface text.
- Do not interpolate or remove supplied frames without approval.
- Desktop uses the complete canvas scrub sequence.
- Mobile uses the art-directed final poster because no portrait frame set was supplied.
- Reduced motion uses a static poster and keeps all content and controls functional.
